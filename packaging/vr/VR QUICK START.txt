VR QUICK START: what to set and press
=====================================

BEFORE PLAY
  Start Virtual Desktop / Link / SteamVR first and put the headset on. A headset that isn't on
  your head holds the game back (5-10 FPS).
  Use Virtual Desktop or SteamVR. On Meta Link the picture judders in True stereo (not solved).
  Set the headset's refresh rate a little BELOW the FPS your PC holds, in Virtual Desktop / Link /
  SteamVR. The game follows it. If the headset runs faster than the game, moving things look double.

LAUNCHER: THE VR TAB (the test PC's settings)
  VR headset            ON
  Stereo mode           True stereo (both eyes every frame; 55-60 FPS on the test PC)
                        AFR: one eye per frame, needs about 90-100 FPS or more
  Headset picture scale 110% (100% = the size your headset's app asks for, pixel for pixel;
                        more = cleaner, slower)
  Head smoothing        Off for steady tracking, Light (default), Strong (lags a little)
  Anti-aliasing in VR   TAA (game's, soft) / Off (sharp, jagged) / SMAA (sharp, smooth edges)
                        / DLSS (RTX, steadiest far away; its quality on the next row)
  Glow in VR            20% (the game's bloom looks like a film over the edges in the headset)
  Exposure in VR        0; + brighter, - darker
  Foveated rendering    Medium (a few FPS when the graphics card is the limit)
  Edge margin           Off (only for frame generation on the PC)

  With VR on the launcher handles and hides: frame rate, output and render resolution, aspect
  ratio, field of view, High-FPS camera fix, dynamic resolution, motion blur, lock to horizon,
  camera shake and the Display tab's anti-aliasing. Your choices come back with VR off.
  Two-thread rendering: as the launcher sets it (on, but OFF on AMD cards)
  FXAA: a switch on the Detail tab, adds to any anti-aliasing
  Mirror and reflection updates: Live (Static only if the FPS is too low)
  Virtual Desktop: H.264+ or HEVC 8-bit (HEVC 10-bit can stutter on a Quest 2)

CAMERAS
  Cockpit, bonnet, bumper, dash and chase all follow your head, in both stereo modes.

KEYS (number row; the numpad works too)
  0        recentre: sit as you drive, look straight ahead, press 0
           (it also recentres by itself when you get into the car)
  2 / 1    seat up / down
  5 / 6    seat left / right
  4 / 3    seat forward / back
           2 cm per press, hold Ctrl for 10 cm
           The seat is saved automatically and stays for the next launch.
           To reset it, delete data\user\vr_seat.ini
  [ / ]    the picture darker / brighter
  F12      the FPS on the monitor
  Every other key is the game's (the development keys need dev_keys = 1 in vr.ini).

IF SOMETHING IS WRONG
  Menu at your feet or the view turned sideways: press 0
  "xrGetSystem ... failed (-35)" in the log with Virtual Desktop running: reconnect in
           Virtual Desktop on the headset and start the game again
  Low FPS: lower the Headset picture scale, Foveated rendering Medium or Strong,
           check Dynamic resolution is off, close background downloads (game launchers,
           torrents), Wallpaper Engine and overlays: the game is CPU-limited
  Bugs and FPS reports: send the log from the logs folder, plus the headset, the connection
  (Virtual Desktop / Link / SteamVR), CPU, GPU, headset picture scale and anti-aliasing
