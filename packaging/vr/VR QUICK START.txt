VR QUICK START: what to set and press
=====================================

BEFORE PLAY
  Start Virtual Desktop / Link / SteamVR first and put the headset on. A headset that isn't on
  your head holds the game back (5-10 FPS).
  Each eye gets half the game's FPS: you need about 90-100 FPS or more in the game to be
  comfortable (the game is heavy on the CPU).

LAUNCHER (the test PC's settings)
  Display   Frame rate: Unlimited
            Output / Render resolution: Default, Widescreen: Off, Widescreen FOV fix: off
            Two-thread rendering: on
            VR headset: ON, Headset picture: 2688x2880
              (2496x2688 for weaker PCs, 3200x3440 and up for Quest 3 / Pimax)
            Anti-aliasing: Game or DLSS (RTX). NEVER FSR in VR (it mixes the eyes)
            Picture: brightness / contrast / gamma work in the headset too
  Graphics  everything off / Game, Dynamic resolution OFF, Motion blur OFF
  Gameplay  Mirror and reflection updates: Live
              (Static only if the FPS is too low: ~10% faster, but frozen reflections and rain
              on the glass, a bluish dashboard reflection on the windscreen)
            Patches: Disable motion blur and depth of field ON,
                     High-FPS camera workaround off
  Driving   Field of view: MUST BE 100% (VR sets the view itself)
            Camera shake: 0-30% (0 = none); Lock to horizon: no effect in the cockpit with VR
  Virtual Desktop: 120 Hz, H.264+ or HEVC 8-bit (HEVC 10-bit can stutter on a Quest 2)

KEYS (number row; the numpad works too)
  0        recentre: sit as you drive, look straight ahead, press 0
           (it also recentres by itself when you get into the car)
  2 / 1    seat up / down
  5 / 6    seat left / right
  4 / 3    seat forward / back
           2 cm per press, hold Ctrl for 10 cm
           The seat is saved automatically and stays for the next launch.
           To reset it, delete data\user\vr_seat.ini
  7        DLSS model (only with DLSS on)
  8 / 9    experimental modes, leave them off
  F10      don't press it in VR (it switches to FSR)

IF SOMETHING IS WRONG
  Menu at your feet or the view turned sideways: press 0
  Low FPS: pick a smaller headset picture, check Dynamic resolution is off
           close background downloads (game launchers, torrents), Wallpaper Engine and
           overlays: the game is CPU-limited, every background app costs frames
  Bugs and FPS reports: send the log from the logs folder, plus the headset, the connection
  (Virtual Desktop / Link / SteamVR), CPU, GPU, headset picture size and anti-aliasing
